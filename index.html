Four Pillars of Absolutism

Core World History Unit Concepts

1. Centralization of Power

Absolute monarchs replaced fragmented feudal lords with royal bureaucrats (like French intendants), professional standing armies, and uniform tax systems.

2. The Divine Right of Kings

Thinkers like Bishop Bossuet argued monarchs answered solely to God—making political rebellion both a treasonous crime and a mortal sin.

3. Cultural & Economic Control

Monumental palaces (Versailles, El Escorial, St. Petersburg), state art academies, and mercantilist tariffs projected prestige and bound elites to the throne.

4. Catalyst for Revolution

Unchecked royal taxation, religious intolerance, and costly wars provoked Enlightenment philosophers (Locke, Voltaire, Rousseau) and democratic revolutions.

Royal Rules & How to Win

Campaign Goal: You must play all 4 European Monarchs (Louis XIV, Peter the Great, Philip II, and Louis VIII) and win at least 3 out of 4 reigns to unlock your official Royal Decree PDF.

  • How to Win a Monarch's 12-Turn Reign: You must reach the end of Turn 12 with at least 3 of your 5 Crowns remaining AND all three meters (Treasury, Stability, Divine Legitimacy) at 35 or higher.
  • How Crowns Are Lost:
    • If any meter drops below 30 at the end of a turn, you lose 1 Crown.
    • If any meter hits 0, you lose 2 Crowns right away (and that meter resets to 15).
    • If two or more meters are below 35 at the same time, kingdom-wide crisis costs an extra 1 Crown.
